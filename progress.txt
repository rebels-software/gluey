### Implementation

  - int32_be, int32_le: signed 32-bit (big/little endian)
  - uint32_be, uint32_le: unsigned 32-bit (big/little endian)
  - float32_be, float32_le: IEEE 754 float (big/little endian)
- Creates JsonDocument with extracted fields as result
